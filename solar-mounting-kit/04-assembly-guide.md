# GM650 — Site Assembly Guide

Crew of 2. Tools: string line, level, tape, cordless drill (Ø9, Ø11 bits), M8 rivnut
setter, torque wrench, sockets 13/17/19 mm, shovel/auger, wheelbarrow or mini-mixer.

## 1. Set out & foundations (day 1)

1. Choose open ground, row running **east–west**, panels facing **north**, tilt down
   to the north. Avoid shading 9 am–3 pm year-round.
2. String two parallel lines **2430 mm apart** (front and rear footing lines).
3. Mark trestle positions: outer trestles under the outer panel edges, interior
   trestles at **≈1150 mm centres** (n panels → n + 1 trestles).
4. Dig pads 500 × 500 × 450 deep; form up 150 mm above grade (total pad depth 600).
5. Pour 20 MPa concrete. Set 2 × M12 galv J-bolts per pad at **100 mm centres**,
   projecting 60 mm, using the baseplate (or a ply template) as a jig. Check the
   bolt pairs across the row with the string line — get this right, everything
   else bolts up easily. Cure 7 days (or use M12 chemical studs after 2 days).

## 2. Trestles

1. Place a 3 mm EPDM pad on each footing, stand each trestle over its J-bolts,
   **rafter sloping down toward the north (front/low side)**.
2. Level and align with the string line; nut + washer, snug only.

## 3. Rails

1. Lay the three rail lines on the rafters: positions **540 / 1273 / 2005 mm** from
   the rafter's front (low) tip to each rail centreline.
2. Join segments with GM-S01 sleeves — 4 × M8×70 through-bolts per splice. Keep
   splices clear of trestle rafters where practical (anywhere mid-bay is fine).
3. At every trestle, drill Ø11 vertically through rail and rafter; fit
   M10×120 through-bolt, nyloc underneath. Torque 30 Nm.
4. Trim the last rail segments so rails overhang the final panel edge by ~60 mm.
5. Fully tighten all splice bolts (20 Nm) and re-tension the row against the line.

## 4. Bracing

Fit crossed GM-B01 straps in the **first bay, last bay, and every 4th bay**: bolt
strap ends to the rear-post top of one trestle and the chord end of the next
(M10×20, holes site-drilled Ø11). Tension by hand before final tightening.

## 5. Panels

1. Set M8 rivnuts in the rail top face: at the row end and at each panel joint,
   one per rail (drill Ø11, set rivnut). Use the supplied drilling template.
2. Stand the first panel (2 people — 29.6 kg), portrait, bottom edge overhanging the
   lower rail equally to the top rail overhang. Fit **3 end clamps** on the outer edge.
3. Fit **3 mid clamps** on the trailing edge, place the next panel against them,
   repeat. Finish the row with 3 end clamps. Torque clamp bolts **16 Nm** (check the
   clamp supplier's figure). Serrated washers under every clamp bolt for earthing.
4. Clamps must sit within JA Solar's approved clamp zones (rails at 0.2 L / 0.5 L /
   0.8 L achieve this) — do not move rails without checking the JA installation manual.

## 6. Earthing & electrical

Bond each rail line with an M8 earth lug and 6 mm² Cu to the array earth per
AS/NZS 5033. Cable management: cable ties to rails, drip loops at the junction boxes.
Electrical installation by a licensed electrician.

## 7. Final checks

- All nylocs torqued; splice bolts 20 Nm, M10 30 Nm, anchors 45 Nm, clamps 16 Nm.
- No panel corner unsupported; end overhangs ≤ 60 mm.
- Re-torque after the first month, then annually (pre-cyclone-season check).
