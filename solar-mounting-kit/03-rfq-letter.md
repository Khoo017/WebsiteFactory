- **Jig:** trestles must be welded in a fixture so all frames are interchangeable.
  Please include jig cost as a separate one-time line if applicable.
- **First article:** 1 trestle + 2 rails + 1 sleeve for my inspection and a test

- **Welder qualification:** welders experienced in structural aluminium; please note
  your relevant experience (boat building, structural, etc.).
- **Finish:** mill finish, welds wire-brushed. No anodising or painting.
- **Packing:** parts bundled per kit as per the packing list in the BOM document.
