# GM650 Cyclone Ground Mount Kit

Modular ground-mount bracket + rail kit for **JA Solar JAM72S42-LR** (625–650 W) modules,
designed for fabrication by a general aluminium workshop in Suva, Fiji.

| | |
|---|---|
| Module | JA Solar JAM72S42-LR, 2465 × 1134 × 30 mm, 29.6 kg |
| Orientation | Portrait, single row, facing north |
| Tilt | 10° fixed |
| Bundle sizes | 1 to 12 panels per row |
| Wind design | 70 m/s (252 km/h) ultimate 3-s gust — cyclonic, AS/NZS 1170.2 Region C basis |
| Structure | Welded 6061-T6 aluminium SHS trestles + spliced SHS rails |
| Fasteners | Stainless A4/316 throughout; hot-dip galv cast-in anchors |
| Foundations | Cast-in-place concrete pads, 2 per trestle |

## How the modularity works

Two SKUs cover every bundle size:

- **SK — Starter Kit** (one per row): 1 trestle, 6 end clamps, 1 bracing set, anchors.
- **PK — Panel Kit** (one per panel): 1 trestle, 3 × 1250 mm rail segments, 3 splice
  sleeves, 3 mid clamps, anchors and fasteners.

**A bundle of *n* panels = 1 × SK + n × PK** (n = 1…12). Every added panel adds one
trestle and extends the three rail lines by one spliced segment. Add **1 extra bracing
set (BK) per 4 panels**. Nothing is bespoke per bundle size — the fabricator makes two
box types, at any volume.

| Bundle | SK | PK | BK | Trestles | Footings | Concrete | Approx. aluminium |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 panel | 1 | 1 | – | 2 | 4 | 0.6 m³ | 33 kg |
| 2 panels | 1 | 2 | – | 3 | 6 | 0.9 m³ | 51 kg |
| 4 panels | 1 | 4 | – | 5 | 10 | 1.5 m³ | 88 kg |
| 6 panels | 1 | 6 | 1 | 7 | 14 | 2.1 m³ | 126 kg |
| 8 panels | 1 | 8 | 1 | 9 | 18 | 2.7 m³ | 162 kg |
| 12 panels | 1 | 12 | 2 | 13 | 26 | 3.9 m³ | 238 kg |

Row length ≈ n × 1.155 m (12 panels ≈ 13.9 m). Bundles larger than 12 = start a second row.
